# Bug hunt: first pass (2026-10-12)

**How it was done:** an AI (not a human) drove the real local app on a scratch data folder, with
synthetic resumes only and the real writing model (Codex). No personal data was used. This is
**not** user testing; it finds bugs, it doesn't show whether people like the product.

**What passed:** import a Word resume, search (63 real jobs came back), choose a job, tailor with
the real model (24 s, validation PASS, one-page PDF), download the PDF and Word file, Apply and
Tracker pages load.

## Bugs found

**Status:** #1–#4 fixed the same day, test-first, and re-checked through the real import (see the commit "fix: read one-line education…").

| # | What a person sees | Severity | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Education is misread when written on one line.** "B.S. in Economics, State University, 2019" ends up entirely in the *school* box. The degree and field are empty, and Career Profile then asks the person to fix a degree they did state. | High: it's the most common layout, so almost every user hits it | 3 of 4 education layouts tried failed. The fixture resume itself ("BS Economics, State University, 2019") showed "the degree wasn't recognized". |
| 2 | **A resume with two degrees loses both.** "M.S. … , 2022" and "B.A. … , 2019" each land in the school box. | High, same cause as #1 | Tried with a Master's and a Bachelor's. |
| 3 | **Job entries written "Company \| City \| Dates" with the title on the next line are swapped.** The title shows "Acme Corp" and the employer shows "Portland". | Medium | One layout tried. |
| 4 | **Two-line education leaves the year and city in the school name**, and the GPA is merged into the field ("Economics, GPA 3.6"). | Low | One layout tried. |

## Product finding (not a bug, but it matters)

- **The model proposed 0 changes** on the synthetic resume, for the job tried. So the
  "tailored" resume was identical to the original. The earlier 2026-10-07 and 2026-10-11 real-model
  runs did the same.
- **Unknown:** whether that is right (the resume already matches) or the tailoring is too timid.
  The fixture resume is small, and I can't use a real resume. A person testing with their own
  resume will answer this faster than I can.
- **Related decision:** you chose to allow keyword and adjacent-skill additions (Q9, levels 1–3).
  That's not built yet. It may be the change that makes tailoring visibly do something.

## Not covered yet

PDF resumes, resumes with a header table, non-US formats, long resumes, the Gmail sync, phone
layout by eye, and what the final PDF *looks* like. The Streamlit app wasn't tested.

## Cause of #1–#4

The importer (`product/resume_tailorer/parsers/resume_parser.py`, `_extract_education` and the
work-entry reader) assumes one specific layout. It has no fallback for other common ones.

## Second pass (same day): review findings checked, plus PDF and table resumes

Each bug below was first reproduced with a failing test, then fixed. The fix commit names the cause.

| # | What a person sees | Cause | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | **A new tailoring run is silently replaced by the old review** when a decision is saved in another tab at the same moment. | A new run's save, and "Start over", wrote the review without the per-user lock that decisions use. | Fixed; race reproduced 3/3 by pausing a real decision mid-save. |
| 6 | **"Start over" is undone** by a decision saved in another tab at the same moment. | Same cause as #5. | Fixed. |
| 7 | **AI-assisted review loses the whole run** when rebuilding after its automatic choices fails (for example, a free-form page limit). | That rebuild was inside the run's error handling, so any error failed the run. | Fixed: the review is kept, assistance's choices are dropped, and a note explains why. Reproduced at the run's control flow; the real trigger wasn't recreated end to end. |
| 8 | **"Accept selected" fails without saying which change is the problem**, and a single Accept is told about a "batch". | The error didn't name the change. | Fixed: the error names the change. Accepting stays all-or-nothing, as spec 019 requires. |
| 9 | **PDF resumes saved from Word lose every bullet point.** | U+F0B7, Word's PDF bullet, wasn't recognized as a bullet. | Fixed. |
| 10 | **A resume PDF made by Job Copilot loses every bullet point when it is imported again.** | The PDF reader returns its bullet as U+007F. | Fixed in the importer. The generated PDF itself is fine: PyMuPDF reads the bullet as "•". |
| 11 | **With the name and contact details in a table, which many templates use, the name became "SUMMARY" and the email and phone were missing.** | Word import skipped tables, and the name finder accepted a section heading. | Fixed: tables are read in order, and headings are never taken as a name. |
| 12 | **The summary started with the word "SUMMARY"** when the contact block ran over several lines. | The summary reader only skipped headings near the top. | Fixed (found while fixing #11). |

**Low-severity follow-ups:**

- **Fixed:** selection checkboxes are now named after their change for screen readers ("Select 1. Work experience · …").
- **Fixed:** each decision read and decoded the whole saved review twice. Now it's once (test counts the reads).
- **Not changed, on purpose:** "duplicated word splitting". The same simple regex is used in 5 places, each with its own filtering. It's not a shared helper that drifted, and merging them changes nothing a person sees.
- **Not a bug:** Job Copilot's generated PDFs show their bullet as an odd character only to one PDF library (pypdf). PyMuPDF reads it as "•", so employer systems aren't shown a broken bullet. The importer now accepts both (#10).

## Third pass: found by the spec 022 measurement runs (real model, synthetic resumes)

| # | What a person sees | Cause | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 13 | **An ordinary Word resume can't be tailored at all.** Every run fails in seconds with "contact line missing or ambiguous". | The Word sync's phone pattern matched a date range ("2018 - 2020"), so the resume seemed to have two phone numbers. | Fixed: a phone needs at least 10 digits. Re-checked through the real app, 3/3 runs now pass. |
| 14 | **The tailored Word resume shows "((555) 010-0199"** after the phone is synced. | The phone pattern started at the first digit, so the replacement kept the old "(" and added another. | Fixed: the bracket belongs to the number. Test covers both an edited and an unchanged phone. |
| 15 | **Tailoring changes nothing, with no explanation, until facts are confirmed in Career Profile.** | Unconfirmed facts don't count as evidence, so the model is told there is nothing to make clearer, and even clearly shown skills go on its "do not add" list. Tailor doesn't warn before the run. | Open; it becomes part of spec 022. Proof: the model answered "keep" for every bullet with an empty "make clearer" list. |
| 16 | **A rare server error, or a briefly missing review, batch or run status, while tailoring runs (Windows).** | Reading a file at the instant another thread replaces it raises PermissionError on Windows. The profile reader crashed; the review, batch and run-status readers treated it as "missing" or "idle". Found as an intermittent API test failure. | Fixed: one shared reader retries that brief contention. Reproduced with two-thread tests (about 106 of 3,000 reads failed), all now 0. |
