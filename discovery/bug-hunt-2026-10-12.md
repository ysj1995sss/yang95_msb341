# Bug hunt: first pass (2026-10-12)

**How it was done:** an AI (not a human) drove the real local app on a scratch data folder, with
synthetic resumes only and the real writing model (Codex). No personal data was used. This is
**not** user testing; it finds bugs, it doesn't show whether people like the product.

**What passed:** import a Word resume, search (63 real jobs came back), choose a job, tailor with
the real model (24 s, validation PASS, one-page PDF), download the PDF and Word file, Apply and
Tracker pages load.

## Bugs found

**Status:** #1–#4 fixed the same day, test-first, and re-checked through the real import (see the commit "fix: read one-line education…").

| # | What a person sees | Severity | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Education is misread when written on one line.** "B.S. in Economics, State University, 2019" ends up entirely in the *school* box. The degree and field are empty, and Career Profile then asks the person to fix a degree they did state. | High: it's the most common layout, so almost every user hits it | 3 of 4 education layouts tried failed. The fixture resume itself ("BS Economics, State University, 2019") showed "the degree wasn't recognized". |
| 2 | **A resume with two degrees loses both.** "M.S. … , 2022" and "B.A. … , 2019" each land in the school box. | High, same cause as #1 | Tried with a Master's and a Bachelor's. |
| 3 | **Job entries written "Company \| City \| Dates" with the title on the next line are swapped.** The title shows "Acme Corp" and the employer shows "Portland". | Medium | One layout tried. |
| 4 | **Two-line education leaves the year and city in the school name**, and the GPA is merged into the field ("Economics, GPA 3.6"). | Low | One layout tried. |

## Product finding (not a bug, but it matters)

- **The model proposed 0 changes** on the synthetic resume, for the job tried. So the
  "tailored" resume was identical to the original. The earlier 2026-10-07 and 2026-10-11 real-model
  runs did the same.
- **Unknown:** whether that is right (the resume already matches) or the tailoring is too timid.
  The fixture resume is small, and I can't use a real resume. A person testing with their own
  resume will answer this faster than I can.
- **Related decision:** you chose to allow keyword and adjacent-skill additions (Q9, levels 1–3).
  That's not built yet. It may be the change that makes tailoring visibly do something.

## Not covered yet

PDF resumes, resumes with a header table, non-US formats, long resumes, the Gmail sync, phone
layout by eye, and what the final PDF *looks* like. The Streamlit app wasn't tested.

## Cause of #1–#4

The importer (`product/resume_tailorer/parsers/resume_parser.py`, `_extract_education` and the
work-entry reader) assumes one specific layout. It has no fallback for other common ones.
