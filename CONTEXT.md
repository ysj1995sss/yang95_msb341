# Job Copilot

Job Copilot helps a job seeker find real job postings, produce a resume tailored to each one
from their own verified history, and apply. This glossary fixes one word per concept.

## People and their history

**Job seeker**:
The person using Job Copilot to find and apply for jobs.
_Avoid_: User (in product copy), candidate, applicant

**Builder**:
The person who makes and runs Job Copilot.
_Avoid_: Admin, owner

**Career Profile**:
The job seeker's own work history, education, skills and preferences, stored once and reused for every job.
_Avoid_: Master resume, account, experience bank

**Fact**:
One item in the Career Profile (a job, a bullet, a degree, a skill) that the job seeker stands behind.
_Avoid_: Claim, data point

**Resume file**:
The Word or PDF document the job seeker imported; each import is a new version.
_Avoid_: Original, upload, master

## Jobs

**Job**:
One real posting from an employer, found on a job board or pasted in by the job seeker.
_Avoid_: Listing, role, opening

**Board**:
An employer's public jobs page on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby or SmartRecruiters that Job Copilot searches.
_Avoid_: Source, feed

**Fit**:
How closely a Job's requirements match the Career Profile, shown as a percentage.
_Avoid_: Match score, ATS score

## Tailoring

**Tailoring run**:
One pass that writes Proposed changes to fit a Job and builds a Tailored resume from them.
_Avoid_: Generation, optimization

**Proposed change**:
One suggested edit to one part of the resume, with the original wording, the new wording and the reason.
_Avoid_: Suggestion, diff, edit

**Change level**:
How far a Proposed change goes beyond the Facts: (1) reword or reorder, (2) add an unlisted skill the job seeker has, (3) add an adjacent skill, (4) invent something. Levels 1–3 are allowed; level 4 never is.
_Avoid_: Aggressiveness, creativity

**Review**:
The job seeker's pass over the Proposed changes of one Tailoring run, deciding each one.
_Avoid_: Approval, editing session

**Decision**:
What happened to one Proposed change: Accepted, Kept original, or Edited by the job seeker.
_Avoid_: Disposition, status

**AI-assisted review**:
A Review in which Job Copilot decides the clearly safe Proposed changes first and leaves the rest to the job seeker.
_Avoid_: Auto-review, AI approval

**Checks**:
The tests every Tailored resume must pass before it can be used: readable, the right length, and nothing unsupported.
_Avoid_: Validation (in product copy), ATS check

**Tailored resume**:
The finished Word and PDF files for one Job, usable only if they passed the Checks.
_Avoid_: Artifact, output

**Out of date**:
A Tailored resume made before the Career Profile last changed; it must be tailored again before it is used.
_Avoid_: Stale, expired

**Batch**:
Up to 10 Jobs tailored one after another in the background.
_Avoid_: Bulk run, queue

## Applying

**Ready to apply**:
A Job whose Tailored resume passed the Checks and is up to date, waiting to be sent.
_Avoid_: Prepared, queued

**Apply**:
Send an application for a Job to the employer, either by the job seeker on the employer's site or by Auto-apply.
_Avoid_: Submit (in product copy)

**Auto-apply** _(planned)_:
Job Copilot sending applications on the job seeker's behalf without asking per Job, within the Daily cap.
_Avoid_: Autopilot, bot, mass apply

**Daily cap** _(planned)_:
The most applications Auto-apply may send in one day.

**Sent log** _(planned)_:
The record of every application Auto-apply sent, including any level 2–3 Proposed changes in it.
_Avoid_: History, audit trail

**Tracker**:
The job seeker's list of Jobs they have applied to or plan to, with each one's current stage.
_Avoid_: Pipeline, CRM
