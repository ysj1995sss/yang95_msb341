Non-Negotiable Principle
Optimize presentation, never manufacture qualifications.

The system can rephrase, reorganize, strengthen wording, and highlight existing experience differently. It can never invent experience, skills, certifications, numbers, responsibilities, or change factual dates/employers. Every change must be truthful and verifiable from the original resume.
