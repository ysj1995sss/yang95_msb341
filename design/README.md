# Design

Flows, screens, and what happened when someone tried to use it.

- Sketches, wireframes, and screenshots of what you built
- `usability-tests/`: one file per session: what you asked them to do, what they actually
  did, where they hesitated or got stuck

Watching one person use your thing tells you more than any amount of guessing about it. Write
down what they did, not what they said about it.
