# Closer

Closer is a free app of conversation questions and games for couples, friends, families and
teams. It runs in the browser and can be installed to a phone's home screen. This glossary fixes
one word per concept.

## What people play

**Experience**:
One of the seven things a person picks on Explore, such as Imagination & Storytelling. It has a name, a color and a number of questions.
_Avoid_: Category (in product copy), mode, section

**Question group**:
One of the ten original sets the questions are written in. An Experience plays one or more question groups; three Experiences join two groups each.
_Avoid_: Category, library group

**Question**:
One prompt to read aloud. Most are open questions; Trivia questions have four answers and one right one.
_Avoid_: Card, prompt

**Depth**:
How far a question goes, from 1 (Fun and light) to 4 (Bold). In Trivia the same four steps mean Easy to Expert.
_Avoid_: Level, difficulty (except in Trivia)

**Round**:
One shuffled run through an Experience's questions. A round can be left and continued later.
_Avoid_: Session, game (a Game is something else)

**Game**:
A way to play together that is not a plain round of questions: Guess My Answer, Debate Night, Surprise me.
_Avoid_: Mode, activity

## What people keep

**Library**:
The person's own things, all on their device: Favorites, My questions and Seasonal packs.
_Avoid_: Saved, collection

**Favorite**:
A question the person hearted to keep.
_Avoid_: Bookmark, like

**My question**:
A question the person wrote themselves.
_Avoid_: Custom question, user question

**Pack**:
A set of ten questions for one occasion, such as Valentine's or Gratitude Table. Packs "in season" come first.
_Avoid_: Seasonal set, bundle

## Using it on a phone

**Installed app**:
Closer opened from a home-screen icon rather than a browser tab.
_Avoid_: PWA, native app

**Tab bar**:
The row of main places (Home, Explore, Games, Library) fixed to the bottom of the screen on a phone. It is hidden while a round or Game is being played.
_Avoid_: Bottom nav, footer
