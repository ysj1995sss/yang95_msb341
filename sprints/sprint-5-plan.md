# Sprint 5 Plan

**Goal:** Get input from 4 people on using Closer installed on their phones, and make the installed experience work better on phones based on what they say.

**Why this:** It will make the user experience better. Tester feedback was that nobody is going to use the website, but they would use an app. I don't have time to make an app, so making installing Closer to the home screen easier and smoother is the right next step to address that concern.

**Done looks like:** All 4 testers install Closer to their home screen. After installing, each one goes through the app and tests it. I fix the features they don't like.

**Predicted difficulty:** 3

**Changes during the sprint:**
- Nov 16 to 17: scope grew. Besides the phone app pass (tab bar, install card, update notice), I redesigned the site and added 16 new games, which were not in the original plan.
- Nov 23: made changes from the four testers' feedback (Games filter, Help page, Back keeps your place, a fresh shuffle each time the app opens, a compact depth menu on small phones). Some suggestions were held for later: the welcome tour, a gentler default for teams and families, export and import, and the name.

**Actual difficulty:** 3

**Why it differed:** It didn't. The hardest part was getting feedback from people, learning from it, and working out what to improve and fine-tune.

**Retro:** I believe I hit the goal: four different people gave feedback, and I made changes from it. Next sprint, I'll get feedback from testers who install it on their phones, and see what I can do to improve the native phone app experience.
