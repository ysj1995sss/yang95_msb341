# Sprint 1 Plan

**Goal:** Build a working MVP with a basic web form that takes a user's resume and a job description, identifies missing or relevant keywords, tailors the resume while keeping the user's experience truthful, and exports a polished one-page PDF.

**Why this:** Resume tailoring is the most repetitive part of job applications and the foundation for the larger product. It forces me to build and test the core capabilities—parsing the user's experience, analyzing job requirements, and matching them—before adding job search and auto-apply integrations. It is small enough to validate quickly, and the same resume parsing, job-description analysis, and keyword matching engine can power future features.

**Done looks like:** A working MVP that achieves 80% keyword and qualification alignment on 8 out of 10 real job postings, reduces the resume-tailoring process to under 5 minutes per application, and can be tested by at least 3 other job seekers who successfully generate a tailored resume without my help.

**Predicted difficulty:** 4
